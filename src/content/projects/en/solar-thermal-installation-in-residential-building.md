This project involved the implementation of a solar thermal collection system designed to meet the domestic hot water (DHW) needs of a residential building.

Given the surroundings and the characteristics of the property, priority was given to an architectural integration that respected the building’s aesthetics and minimised visual impact. To this end, evacuated-tube solar collectors were chosen: a highly efficient solution in thermal terms that can be adapted to the existing architectural design. This type of collector not only delivers excellent energy performance but also blends harmoniously into its surroundings, offering a sustainable and visually discreet solution.
