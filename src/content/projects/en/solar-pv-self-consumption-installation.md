**Location:** Cala Saona, Formentera

This project involved the implementation of a solar PV self-consumption installation for a tourist establishment, with the aim of optimising the complex’s energy consumption by harnessing solar energy.

From the outset, priority was given to careful architectural integration, ensuring that the solar panels blended harmoniously with the building’s design, respecting the aesthetics of the surroundings and improving its sustainability. The installation not only significantly reduces the complex’s dependence on the conventional electricity grid, but also helps to strengthen its image as an environmentally committed establishment. Thanks to the choice of latest-generation materials and technologies, the system achieves optimum performance while minimising its visual impact, offering a solution that combines functionality, sustainability and design.
