**Location:** Eivissa (Ibiza)

This project involved the audit and commissioning of an industrial refrigeration installation designed to meet the demanding ice production and storage requirements of a high-output factory. The installation has a total installed compressor capacity of 189 kW, distributed across a system of latest-generation compressors. The main objective of the project was to ensure the efficient, safe and sustainable operation of the process and storage cold rooms, which are essential to preserve the quality and properties of the product at every stage of the cold chain.
