Alongside professional practice in the private sector, our work also extends to **teaching and science communication**, sharing a wide range of technical documentation with other professionals and companies in the sector.

In 2013 a series of materials was developed, aimed mainly at training in areas related to teaching and professional practice, and a first volume was published as a support document for students and teachers in different settings: both formal education (secondary schools and vocational training centres) and state-subsidised or private centres, especially courses leading to the recognition of units of competence through the accreditation of professional experience. These materials also support the training of technicians working in private companies.
