## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores in the user’s browser. This policy also covers other similar technologies for storing information on the device (such as the browser’s local storage), which are referred to collectively below as “cookies”.

## 2. Cookies used on this website

This website **does not set any cookies of its own** and does not use analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies. The pages are served as static files and do not load fonts, videos, maps or any other third-party content.

The only exception is the contact page, described below.

## 3. Cloudflare Turnstile on the contact page

On the [contact](/en/contact/) page (and its Spanish and Catalan versions), the form includes Cloudflare Turnstile, a service provided by Cloudflare, Inc. that checks that the message is being sent by a person and not by an automated program (spam protection).

To do this, Turnstile may use strictly necessary technical storage in your browser and process technical data about your connection and browser. This storage:

- only takes place on the contact page;
- is used solely to secure the form and prevent automated submissions;
- is not used for analytics or advertising purposes.

For more information, see the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy/) and [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 4. Consent and cookie banner

The storage described above is strictly necessary to provide the service you expressly request (sending the contact form) and to keep the website secure, and is therefore exempt from consent under Article 22(2) of Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE). For this reason, the website does not display a cookie notice or banner.

If cookies requiring consent are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before they are used.

## 5. How to block or delete cookies

You can set your browser to block or delete cookies and local storage; see your browser’s help pages for instructions. If you block technical storage on the contact page, the spam check may not work and you may be unable to submit the form. In that case, you can write directly to [jlcampos@jlc-ingenieros.com](mailto:jlcampos@jlc-ingenieros.com).

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please write to [info@jlc-ingenieros.com](mailto:info@jlc-ingenieros.com).
